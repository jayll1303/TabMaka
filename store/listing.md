## Summary (short description, <= 132 chars)

## Detailed description

Meet a little companion that lives on your new-tab page. It's a procedural
creature, animated entirely with math, so it moves organically as it swims
after your cursor and wanders on its own when you step away.

What you get:
- A companion that follows your cursor in real time and tracks it with its eyes
- Calm wandering behavior when you're not interacting
- Six companions to choose from: eel, fish, snake, tadpole, dog, and frog
- Name your companion and an optional 12/24-hour clock
- A clean, distraction-free new tab

Built for people who want a little delight without the bloat:
- 100% local. No analytics, no telemetry, no network requests, no account.
- The only permission requested is storage, used to remember your companion and settings.
- Lightweight and fast. Vanilla TypeScript on Canvas, no framework, so it loads instantly.
- Respects reduced-motion settings and pauses when the tab is hidden, so a resting companion costs almost no CPU.

Open source (MIT). Build it from source or read the code on GitHub.

## Single-purpose statement (required by Chrome for new-tab overrides)

Companion New Tab has a single purpose: to replace the browser's new-tab page
with an interactive animated companion and an optional clock. It does not
perform any other function.

## Permission justification

- storage: Persists the user's chosen companion, its name, and clock
  preference locally so they are remembered between sessions. No data is
  transmitted off the device.
