# TabMaka v0.1.1 - Jump Hops & Clock Styles 🐸✨

A fun and delightful update for TabMaka with interactive jumping hops, animated drag poses, and curated offline typography!

### 🌟 What's New in v0.1.1

- 🐸 **Dynamic Jump Hop Mechanics**: Click or tap your frog companion to make it hop to a random nearby spot! Includes organic parabolic flight arc, directional facing (turns left/right based on jump direction), and bouncy squash & stretch physics.
- 🖐️ **Animated Drag & Drop**: The frog now assumes a cute ready-to-leap crouch pose while being dragged and smoothly lands with an elastic squash on release.
- ⏰ **Curated Clock Typography**: Fresh collection of offline embedded fonts to customize your clock style (Minimal Sans, Retro Serif, Digital Mono, and Playful Soft).
- 🛠️ **UI & Interaction Polish**: Text selection is disabled during drag operations for a seamless, distraction-free experience.
- 🚀 **Automated CI/CD Release Pipeline**: Seamless automated deployment to the Chrome Web Store on every release.
