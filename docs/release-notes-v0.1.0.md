# TabMaka v0.1.0 - Cozy New Tab Pet 🐸

We are excited to introduce the first official release of **TabMaka** — a lightweight, distraction-free new-tab companion for Chrome and Edge.

### ✨ Highlights & Features

- 🐸 **Adorable Kawaii Frog Mascot**: Lives on your new tab, follows your cursor with its eyes, blinks, and breathes organically.
- 🖐️ **Drag & Drop Placement**: Position your frog anywhere on your screen.
- 👉 **Poke & Bounce Reaction**: Click or tap your companion for playful squash-and-stretch reactions.
- 🎨 **Ambient Pastel Themes**: Seamlessly change background tones with the ambient color palette.
- ⏰ **Clean Minimalist Clock**: 3-state toggle (12-hour, 24-hour, or hidden) and editable greeting text.
- 🔒 **100% Local & Privacy-First**: No tracking, no telemetry, no analytics, no external servers, no account required.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast**: Built with pure Vanilla TypeScript and HTML5 Canvas, zero heavy frameworks, loads instantly.
